STEP UP Bicycle

In the city, a bicycle is often carried up stairs and through narrow corridors, not only ridden. STEP UP is a lightweight urban bicycle designed to be easier to carry and maneuver, without compromising its everyday functionality.

Type
Industrial design student project
Year
2025
Duration
3 months
Tools
SolidWorks, BikeCAD, KeyShot, 3D printing, hand modeling, physical prototyping
Key features04
01 / 04

Ergonomic carrying handle

Positioned close to the bicycle’s center of mass for more balanced and comfortable lifting.

02 / 04

Adjustable shoulder strap

Extends and retracts as needed, allowing the bicycle to be carried over the opposite shoulder.

03 / 04

Quick-release pedal

Can be repositioned during carrying to prevent interference with the rider’s leg.

04 / 04

Folding handlebar

Reduces the bicycle’s width and enables smooth movement through narrow staircases without catching on railings or disturbing other users.
